3. Introduction / Background

📄 Background
  • Domain: AI-driven disaster management, where ML converts weather data into early flood warnings.
  • Motivation: Traditional flood alerts are manual and delayed, often reaching people too late to act.
  • Gap: Real-time weather forecasts and historical rainfall data are rarely combined into one predictive system.
  • Floods are among the most frequent, costly disasters, making AI-based prediction highly relevant today.
  • Rescue Eye uses ML to predict flood risk early, helping save lives and reduce property damage.
💡 Key Note
Rescue Eye is an AI-based disaster management system that predicts rainfall and flood risks using weather data and machine learning, providing early warnings to help save lives and reduce damage.
📊 Flood Vulnerability in India
  • High-Risk River Basins: Over 40 million hectares in India are flood-prone (e.g. Godavari Basin at Kopargaon, Mithi River at Mumbai).
  • Economic Loss: Recurring floods cost over ₹50,000 Crores annually in infrastructure and crop loss.
  • The Value of Early Lead Time: A 4-hour advance flood warning reduces casualties by up to 78%.
  • Pure Software Approach: Requires zero physical IoT sensors, operating dynamically on live open meteorological telemetry.

7. Proposed System Architecture

🏗️ 4-Layer Architecture
  • Layer 1 (UI): Web frontend with location search autocomplete, GPS locator, and GIS map.
  • Layer 2 (Data Orchestrator): Live REST queries to Open-Meteo (Rain), CWC (Rivers), and SRTM DEM (Elevation).
  • Layer 3 (AI & Hydrology Engine): Multi-factor ensemble model, soil saturation index, and data confidence checker.
  • Layer 4 (Tactical Output): Unified Forecast Card, SHAP factor waterfall, GIS hazard polygons, and Drone CV.
💡 Key Note
Decoupled architecture allows instant scaling to any Indian city or village without requiring local physical sensors.
🔄 Step-by-Step System Flow
  • Step 1: User selects location (e.g. Kopargaon, Godavari Basin).
  • Step 2: Data Orchestrator fetches Weather + River + DEM in parallel (< 1.5s).
  • Step 3: AI checks 7-day soil saturation & river capacity ratio.
  • Step 4: Ensemble model computes calibrated Flood Probability (%).
  • Step 5: UI renders Risk Tier, Arrival Time, Map Polygons, and Action Checklist.

8. AI & Hydrological Methodology

⚙️ How the Prediction Works
  • 1. Rainfall Factor (40% Weight): Measures 1h downpour, 24h accumulation, and 7-day soil moisture saturation.
  • 2. River Level Factor (35% Weight): Compares current river gauge height against the official danger mark.
  • 3. Terrain Elevation (15% Weight): Uses Digital Elevation Models (DEM) to identify low-lying depressions prone to pooling.
  • 4. Historical Frequency (10% Weight): Matches past monsoon return periods and flood archives.
  • Probability Formula: Sigmoidal calibration ensures an honest 0–100% curve.
💡 Key Note
Confidence is calculated separately from Risk: if live sensors are delayed, the system honestly lowers its confidence score.

9. Explainable AI & Interactive GIS Map

💡 Explainable AI (Why is Risk High?)
  • Transparent Justification: Tells rescue officers exactly why a warning was triggered.
  • Positive Risk Factors (+):
      • +68 mm heavy rainfall (+28%)
      • River level at 94% capacity (+24%)
      • Low elevation depression (+12%)
  • Negative Mitigating Factors (-):
      • High drainage slope (-8%)
      • Active storm pumping (-6%)
  • No Black-Box Mystery: Builds trust with NDRF and municipal commanders.
💡 Key Note
Officers never have to guess why an alert fired: the AI shows the exact breakdown of physical evidence.
🗺️ Interactive GIS Map Features
  • Red Threat Polygons: Inundation zones with expected flood depths > 1.5m.

10. Drone Aerial Vision & Survivor Triage

🚁 Drone Computer Vision
  • Edge AI Object Detection: Uses TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD directly in the browser.
  • Identifies Critical Targets: Detects stranded persons on rooftops, submerged vehicles, and rescue boats.
  • One-Click Rescue Queue: Clicking a detected victim logs exact GPS coordinates into the dispatch queue.
  • Before vs After Split Slider: Compares pre-flood satellite images with drone footage to calculate water spread (+312%).
  • Automated Urgency Triage: Priority = (VictimCount × 25) + (WaterDepth × 20) + (Isolation × 15).
💡 Key Note
Allows rescue teams to locate stranded victims in minutes without waiting for cell phone calls.
🚑 Evacuation Routing & Dispatch
  • High-Clearance Channels: Directs rescue boats safely along navigable deep channels.
  • Hazard Bypass: Prevents boats from colliding with submerged power lines or trees.
  • Turn-by-Turn Guidance: Routes evacuees to the nearest open relief camp.
  • Runs 100% in Browser: Operates locally even during temporary network disconnections.
